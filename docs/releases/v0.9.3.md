## Upgrading from 0.9.2

No configuration change is needed, and none of your settings change.

There is one new, optional setting: **launch share**, for two or more metered accounts in normal use (a Claude
Enterprise seat and a ChatGPT Enterprise seat, say). If you have those, run `routr setup` and choose Change one
subscription's settings, then Launch share, to set how you want launches split between them. With one metered
account, or none, there is nothing to set, and setup does not ask.

The update reinstalls routr's skills, and three guides changed:
- the main skill describes the `share` and `share_due` fields and how the lead uses them;
- the orchestrator guide says to take `share_due` among suitable metered options;
- the setup guide has the setting and its guided question.

An agent that already loaded routr reads the older guides until it rereads the skill or starts a new session.

If you are upgrading from 0.9.1 or earlier, read the [v0.9.2 notes](https://github.com/sirkirby/routr/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.9.2.md) too.

## Fixed

- **Guided `routr setup` kept answering keys after the decision model's key question.** Before, once you typed a key
  or pressed Enter to skip it, the next menu ignored every key: arrow keys printed `^[[B`, and in the numbered
  (accessible) mode no answer was taken. Someone setting up before they had a key was stuck. Each prompt now takes
  the terminal back itself. (#132)

## New

- **A launch share between metered accounts.** A metered account has no usage windows, so routr has no headroom
  number for it, and two of them tied in the advice: the lead's choice fell to habit. Now you can give each a
  weight: `claude` 3 and `codex` 1 says you want about three workers on Claude for every one on Codex.
  - Set it in guided `routr setup` (Launch share: no share, 1 to 5, or another weight up to 99), or with
    `routr setup --share claude=3 --share codex=1`; `--share claude=off` removes it.
  - It counts only for accounts that read metered and are in normal use, and only when at least two of them can
    take the work. An account without a share counts as 1. Accounts with usage windows are balanced by headroom, as
    before.
  - routr counts its own launches: each worker that took its task, or was left ready, writes one small record (the
    subscription and the time) under `~/.local/state/routr/launch-counts`. `dispatch` and `usage` read the last 7 days.
    Each metered account then shows how far it is below its target, and `share_due` names the one furthest below.
    The lead still chooses model and effort fit first, and takes `share_due` among suitable options or says why not.
  - Counting never fails a launch. If the history can't be read, no share is shown and the advice says why.
    `routr uninstall` removes the records with routr's other state.
  - Not measured: whether leads following `share_due` reach your ratio in practice.
  (#131, #132)

## Verification

- `bun test` on macOS, Linux and Windows. New tests cover:
  - the share arithmetic, ordering and ties;
  - when a share applies, and the guided question's conditions, including a first run;
  - the launch records: the window, the sweep, stray, damaged and unreadable files, linked folders and the uninstall
    lock;
  - counting at launch, and `--share` in setup;
  - the terminal prompts after the key question, in both modes.

  Each failed on the code before its fix.
- Review: each change had an independent review by a routr worker on another model family, and a docs audit, with
  a verification pass on every fix. The design was reviewed before it was built. All findings were fixed.
- By hand: guided setup driven in a real terminal (a herdr pane) with stand-in metered harnesses in a throwaway home.
  It went dead after the key question on 0.9.2; it now answers, and a Launch share chosen there is saved. From
  source in a throwaway home: Claude is due at 9 of 20 launches with weights 3 and 1, and the single-account `usage`
  view shows no share.
- Live: the launch records were written by real launches on the maintainer's machine during development.
- Not measured: a real two-metered-seat machine using the share over days; native Windows by hand (CI only).

## CLI and JSON compatibility

No field or flag was removed or renamed.
- **`dispatch` and `usage`:** `subscriptions.share_due`, and `share` `{ weight, target, used_pct, left, launches, of }`
  on each normal metered row, only when a share applies. Those rows are then ordered by `left` among themselves;
  `headroom`, `usable`, `candidates` and `most_room` are unchanged. `usage <subscription>` never shows a share.
- **`setup`:** new `--share <subscription>=<1-99>|off`; `share` in `config.json` and in `setup --show`.
- **`launch`:** writes one launch record when it ends `prompted` or `ready`; its output does not change.
