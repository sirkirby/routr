## Upgrading from 0.8.2

No configuration change is needed, and nothing to review in `routr setup`: no setting is new or changed.

The update reinstalls routr's skills, and the orchestrator guidance changed in this release (below). An agent that
already loaded routr reads the older wording until it rereads the skill or starts a new session.

## Upgrading from 0.7.0

Read [Upgrading from 0.7.0](https://github.com/sirkirby/routr/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.8.0.md#upgrading-from-070) in
the v0.8.0 notes first: `routr launch` and `routr cleanup` outside herdr changed in 0.8.0.

## Changed

- **The orchestrator guide says how to brief a fix and how to size the review after it.** A fix's brief now states
  the rule the fix must keep everywhere and where else that rule applies, not only the symptom the lead saw. After a
  review, the next round is sized to what it found: a decision that is the user's goes to the user, a change in
  behaviour goes back to the builder, and a trivial fix (one literal, a typo) the lead checks itself instead of
  launching another round. The `routr-orchestrate` skill carries the same line. This came from a lead agent's report
  on one orchestration (#106), where fixes briefed as their symptom took up to three review rounds each; it was
  observed, not measured, and the guide says so.

## Verification

- `bun test` on macOS, Linux and Windows.
- Review: by a routr worker on another model family, which found one unqualified claim; the lead removed it.
- Not measured: whether the new guidance reduces review rounds.

## CLI and JSON compatibility

- No command, flag or JSON field changes. Config, saved advice, the ledger and telemetry: unchanged.
