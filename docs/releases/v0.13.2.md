## The orchestrator guide asks what the result must satisfy

A guide-only release. In "Write the task", the orchestrator guide now asks the lead's brief to say what the result must satisfy: the behaviour that must stay unchanged, the limits each part must keep, and what it must not touch. These sit beside what to do, where, what done looks like, and what is out of scope.

Step 2 adds one sentence: a brief whose `states_check` and `standalone` readings are not `no` may still be incomplete, so check it against that list.

Why: in a measurement of 24 worker runs on three merged pull requests (in `docs/evidence.md`), one task's hurried brief named the change and asked for tests but dropped its requirements. Every worker given it made the same three mistakes, whichever of two models ran it. routr read that brief `standalone` yes and `states_check` unclear, so neither reading asked for a fix. The guide does not name a model.

## Upgrading from 0.13.1

No setup is needed, and your settings do not change. The update reinstalls routr's skills with the new guide text. A lead that already loaded the guide reads the change in a new session.

## How much of this was run

- **The measurement:** the evidence row states it and its limits: three tasks, two runs per cell, one harness, no basic work, no review loop.
- **routr's readings on that brief:** re-asked twice on `jev-1.14.0`.

Not measured: whether the new wording changes what leads write. A candidate routr question for briefs that state no requirements was measured and not adopted. No wording read that hurried brief `no` without also misreading complete briefs, and the real briefs sampled from the maintainer's ledger already stated their requirements.
