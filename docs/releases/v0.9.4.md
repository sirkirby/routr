## Upgrading from 0.9.3

No configuration change is needed. routr now reads briefs with Jev `jev-1.14.0` instead of `jev-1.13.0`. If your
decision model is TypeSafe's Jev without a version named (what `routr setup` writes), you move to 1.14 with the update
and there is nothing to do.

**If your `config.json` names `"model": "jev-1.13.0"`:** that version is no longer offered. routr drops that entry with
a warning, and uses the next decision model in your list, or Jev 1.14 when it was the only one. To follow Jev's new pin,
change that entry's `"model"` to `null` in `config.json`, keeping your other entries. The command the warning gives
removes the old entry and keeps the rest, so it brings Jev back only when nothing else is left. `routr setup --decision
<list>` replaces the whole list, so pass every entry you want, for example `--decision typesafe,cloudflare:clef`.

The update reinstalls routr's skills, and two guides changed:
- the main skill's note on torn readings quotes 1.14's numbers;
- the setup guide quotes 1.14's accuracy and its `sure_at`.

An agent that already loaded routr reads the older guides until it rereads the skill or starts a new session.

If you are upgrading from 0.9.2 or earlier, read the [v0.9.3 notes](https://github.com/sirkirby/routr/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.9.3.md) too.

## Changed

- **The decision model is Jev 1.14.** routr asks `jev-1.14.0` about each brief, with its own measured profile: it
  presents a level as sure from confidence 0.7 (1.13 used 0.8), and the note on a torn reading quotes 1.14's record
  on the 36 real tasks (right 28 times; when wrong, one level too low 7 times and too high once).
  - What changes in the advice: 1.14 reads levels a little lower. On 339 real tasks from the maintainer's own work,
    scored against how the work turned out, it read 35 fewer tasks as strong, over-rated less (9 against 14 of the 329
    where the chosen level was enough) and under-rated no more (10 of the 10 that fell short below strong were read no
    higher, the same as 1.13). On the maintainers' labelled briefs it was slightly lower: right on 81.5 of 104 against
    82.7, pooled over six runs.
  - It is a maintainer decision on mixed gate results, not a clean pass: two three-run gates on the same items
    disagreed, and the same version asked twice moved by about one brief, the size of the whole difference.
    `docs/evidence.md`, "Moving the pin: Jev 1.14", has the numbers.
- **What the gate holds** (CONTRIBUTING.md): a Jev version is judged on the mean of three runs, and on what a lead
  acts on: the level, `standalone`, and the report and reviewer verdicts. Fresh Claude Code and Codex leads rewrote
  the brief when `standalone` read no (12 of 12, 11 of 12), and mostly checked the other facts against the brief
  themselves. The other facts are still in the advice; they are reported, not gated.

## Verification

- `bun test` on macOS, Linux and Windows. The tests expect `jev-1.14.0` as the default, and a case reads
  `jev-1.13.0` asked by name (the gate's override) as unmeasured.
- Review: an independent review by a routr worker on another model family checked every number in the docs against
  the maintainers' saved results, and two verification passes; a docs audit's findings were fixed (its re-check was cut
  short by the auditor's quota, and the code review's re-check covered the fixes).
- Live, from source: `dispatch` read a brief with `jev-1.14.0`, and `doctor` reported it answering. In a throwaway
  home, a config naming `jev-1.13.0` was dropped with the warning and its fix, and `--decision typesafe:jev-1.13.0` was
  refused.
- Not measured: whether the lower levels change how much rework workers need, beyond the 339 recorded tasks.

## CLI and JSON compatibility

No field or flag was removed or renamed.
- **`decision_model`**: with TypeSafe's Jev as the decision model, new advice and its new ledger rows name `jev-1.14.0`;
  `doctor` shows it in `decision[].asked` and, when it answers, `decision[].model`. Other configured providers, and
  ledger rows already written, keep their own.
- **`sure`, `between`, `notes` and `next`**: with no `sure_at` of your own, Jev's bar moves from 0.8 to 0.7, so a reading
  with confidence from 0.7 up to 0.8 is now `sure: true`, without `between` or the torn-reading note and steps. A
  `sure_at` you set yourself still applies.
- **`routr setup --decision typesafe:jev-1.13.0`** is refused as a model routr has not measured; a config entry naming
  it is dropped, with a warning that names a `routr setup --decision` command for the entries that remain.
