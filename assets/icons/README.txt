Routr icon assets

primary-dark/: full Routr wordmark on the original dark cyan-glow tile.
mark-only/: cyan r mark on the original dark bordered tile.
Both treatments include 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, and 16 px PNGs.
favicon.ico contains the mark-only treatment at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px.

Use primary-dark for social/profile images and mark-only for small website icons.
The PNGs retain the sheet background and are opaque.
These are crops of the supplied 1774 x 887 raster sheet, not regenerated artwork.
Primary crop: 456 x 456 px. Mark-only crop: 188 x 188 px.
Exports larger than those crops are upscaled and cannot add original detail.
