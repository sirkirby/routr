## Upgrading from 0.6.0

No configuration change is needed. The update reinstalls routr's skills; an orchestrator session that already loaded
them keeps the old text until it rereads them or you start a new session.

## Highlights

- **An orchestrator hands out research and reviews to pane workers, not to its own subagents.** In long sessions, an
  orchestrator in herdr sent its planning research, PR reviews and triage to its harness's in-process subagents
  (Claude Code's Agent tool) while implementation went to routr workers. Those run on the orchestrator's own
  subscription, out of your sight, with no ledger row. The routr and `routr-orchestrate` skills now say that, in
  herdr, every piece the orchestrator hands out, research, exploration and reviews included, goes to a pane worker
  through `routr dispatch` and `routr launch`, even when it will wait for the result. It does a piece itself only when
  `dispatch`'s `worker` field says `do it yourself`, and keeps for itself turning findings into the plan, your
  decisions, checking results and integrating them.
- **`routr subagent` says who it is for.** It sizes a subagent for a routr worker, or for an agent outside herdr. Its
  `rule` now says so, and that an orchestrator in herdr uses `dispatch` and `launch` instead. An agent outside herdr
  is pointed at only the "Before you spawn a subagent" part of the worker guide.

How it was checked: replays of one real orchestrator session (an Opus lead), cut just before its research handoff and
its review handoff, with only the skill text it had read swapped. The old text sent 0 of 6 through routr; this text
sent 5 of 6 through `routr dispatch`. The sixth found that the test refused `routr launch` and fell back to a subagent.
Three tries per decision point; other harnesses as the orchestrator are not measured.

## CLI and JSON compatibility

No command, flag or JSON field changed. The text of `routr subagent`'s `rule` field and its help line changed.
Telemetry payloads are unchanged.
